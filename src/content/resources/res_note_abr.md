# Auditory Brainstem Response (ABR) Clinical Masterclass

The Auditory Brainstem Response (ABR) is an auditory evoked potential originating from the auditory nerve and auditory brainstem pathway within a 10-millisecond post-stimulus time window.

---

## 1. Primary Neural Generator Sites

* **Wave I**: Distal portion of Cranial Nerve VIII (Auditory Nerve near cochlea)
* **Wave II**: Proximal portion of Cranial Nerve VIII (near brainstem entry)
* **Wave III**: Cochlear Nucleus (Lower Pons)
* **Wave IV**: Superior Olivary Complex / Lateral Lemniscus (Mid Pons)
* **Wave V**: Inferior Colliculus (Midbrain) - *Most robust and clinically significant wave*

---

## 2. Standard Normative Latencies (70 dB nHL Click @ 21.1/sec)

| Wave | Absolute Latency (ms) | Interwave Interval | Normal Range (ms) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Wave I** | ~1.6 ms | **I - III** | ~2.0 ms |
| **Wave III** | ~3.7 ms | **III - V** | ~1.9 ms |
| **Wave V** | ~5.6 ms | **I - V** | ~3.9 - 4.1 ms |

---

## 3. Neurodiagnostic Red Flags for Retrocochlear Pathology

1. **Prolonged Wave V Absolute Latency**: Exceeding normative mean by >2.5 standard deviations.
2. **Interaural Wave V Latency Difference (IT5)**: >0.2 - 0.3 ms difference between ears.
3. **Prolonged I-V Interwave Interval**: Indicates slowed neural conduction along the auditory brainstem.
4. **Morphology Degradation at High Click Rates**: Wave V breakdown when rate increases from 21.1/sec to 81.1/sec.
