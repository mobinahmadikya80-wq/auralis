# Vestibular Diagnostics: VNG & vHIT Comprehensive Guide

## 1. Oculomotor Battery
* **Saccades**: Measures velocity, accuracy, and latency. Dysmetria or slow saccades point toward cerebellar or brainstem lesions.
* **Smooth Pursuit**: Evaluates gain and phase. Decreased gain indicates central vestibulocerebellar pathways involvement.
* **Gaze Nystagmus**: Fixation vs non-fixation nystagmus differentiation (Peripheral nystagmus suppresses with visual fixation).

---

## 2. Caloric Stimulation Protocol
Memory Mnemonic: **COWS** (*Cold Opposite, Warm Same*)
* **Cold Caloric Water/Air (30°C)**: Induces endolymphatic fluid convection causing nystagmus fast-phase toward the **opposite** ear.
* **Warm Caloric Water/Air (44°C)**: Fast-phase toward the **same** ear.
* **Jongkees Formula for Unilateral Weakness (UW)**:
$$\text{UW \%} = \frac{(R_{30} + R_{44}) - (L_{30} + L_{44})}{R_{30} + R_{44} + L_{30} + L_{44}} \times 100$$
* A unilateral weakness $>20 - 25\%$ indicates peripheral horizontal semicircular canal or superior vestibular nerve hypofunction.
